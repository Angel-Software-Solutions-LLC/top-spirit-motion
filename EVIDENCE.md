# Evidence ledger

Every external figure in the briefing at
https://angel-software-solutions-llc.github.io/top-spirit-motion/ carries a
source, a retrieval date and a grade on the slide itself. This file is the
same discipline in one place: each claim, where it comes from, what it does
not say, and the claims we removed because they failed checking. It exists
so a reader, or a reader's assistant, can verify the briefing without
trusting us.

## Grade key

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Statute or audited filing, readable directly |
| B | Company or association reported |
| C | Trade press citing named people |
| D | Vendor published |
| E | Our own offer or inference, no external authority claimed |

## Claims we removed, and why

The circulating beverage-AI evidence base mostly does not survive source
tracing. These claims were in earlier drafts or in circulating decks and
were removed before publication:

| Removed claim | Why |
|---|---|
| Pernod Ricard D-STAR "+1.5 to 4.5% sell-out uplift" | The range is Harvard Business School case wording about Pernod's AI tools generally, by market. Not Pernod's own figure, not tool-specific, not "sell-out". |
| Diageo EDGE "18% fewer out-of-stocks" | Does not appear in any Diageo filing or report we could trace. |
| AB InBev BEES "+8 to 12% basket size" | Metric does not match what AB InBev reports; the company's own disclosures use different measures. |
| "Average German consulting day rate EUR 1,300" and similar market benchmarks | The study exists but is paid, unread by us, and the figure is unverified. We do not quote what we cannot read. |
| A named vendor case study for an Austrian distributor | Vendor-published only, with no independent trace. Best regional fit we found, and it still fails the rule. |

The honest headline behind this table: no European or DACH beverage
distributor publishes a traceable before-and-after AI result at grade C or
better. That absence is a finding. It is why the briefing argues for
generating evidence rather than borrowing it.
