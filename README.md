# Executive fit-and-method briefing

Live deck: **https://angel-software-solutions-llc.github.io/top-spirit-motion/**

Prepared by Angel Software Solutions LLC for a discussion on 17 August 2026.
The deck is intentionally generic and public-safe: it contains no client-provided estate,
staffing, supplier, budget or programme facts.

## What the meeting is meant to decide

By the end of the conversation, either:

1. there is enough mutual fit to scope the first real question, with its sponsor,
   decision owner, working lead, proof, commercial route and scoping date; or
2. the missing proof, stakeholder or decision is explicit, with an owner and a date.

The deck does **not** request approval for a pilot, platform, programme, budget or unpaid
analysis. It presents three possible starting questions for correction rather than
pretending the client has already selected one.

## Evidence and confidentiality

- External factual claims link to their source and state their relevant limitation.
- Client-private disclosures are used to prepare the room, not published as sales copy.
- Technical and commercial unknowns remain questions until the relevant owner confirms them.
- Team-profile claims are narrow and linked; references, capacity, specialist coverage and
  availability remain diligence gates before appointment.

## Presenting

Use the visible controls, arrow keys, swipe or click. Every slide has a stable deep link
from `#s1` through `#s11`, and browser Back is supported.

Motion is preserved at two chapter changes. It is off on a first visit and can be enabled
with the visible **Motion** control or the **T** key. The operating system's reduced-motion
preference is always a veto. The **Print** control produces one 16:9 landscape page per
slide with animations resolved.
