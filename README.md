# Executive AI partnership discussion

The deck: **https://angel-software-solutions-llc.github.io/top-spirit-motion/**

This repository contains a nine-slide executive discussion deck from Angel
Software Solutions. It is designed to support a fit-and-method conversation,
not to imply approval of a platform, programme, pilot, price or architecture.

## What the deck does

- Opens with the decision the meeting can realistically make.
- Presents data, process, adoption and estate priorities as hypotheses for the
  room to correct.
- Separates enterprise strategy from a bounded operating-company proof.
- Defines a decision charter and five gates before any proof begins.
- Frames ERP and data-platform choices as an evidence-led decision area without
  presuming a vendor, migration or licensing answer.
- Shows one conditional knowledge-continuity example without recommending a
  platform.
- Works alongside the current IT partner, makes governance part of adoption,
  and names proposed accountabilities.
- Closes on scope definition only: an executive-ready one-page decision brief
  before the relevant planning gate, with an explicit right to stop.

The deck deliberately contains no client-private disclosures, third-party ROI
claims, client architecture claims, pricing, timing promises or inferred
executive identities.

## Using it

Use the arrow keys, Page Up/Page Down, Space, Home/End, or click the left and
right halves of the screen. Motion clips are **off by default**. Press **T** to
toggle them during a session, or append `?motion=1` to enable them on load.

Printing produces one landscape page per slide. The visible text is live HTML;
photographic backgrounds and optional transitions are local assets.
