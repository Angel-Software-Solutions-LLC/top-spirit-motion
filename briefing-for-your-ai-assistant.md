# Angel Software Solutions: a briefing written for your AI assistant

Prepared 17 August 2026 by Angel Software Solutions LLC for Top Spirit /
Marussia Beverages. Nothing in this document is confidential and you are
welcome to share it onward or feed it to any tool you use.

**Why this document exists.** We were told your team uses an AI assistant to
assess suppliers, and this document is written for that check. So rather
than a brochure, this is a structured briefing written
to be machine-read: claims are separated from offers, every external figure
carries its source, its retrieval date and an evidence grade, and the final
section hands your assistant the questions we think it should be asking us.
Nothing here asks your assistant to conclude anything. Checking is the point.

## 1. What we are proposing

One thing: we write one page, and you choose what it has to decide. That
page names one decision you already have to take, who owns it, what evidence
would settle it, and what would make the answer no. It commits no budget and
binds nobody.

If the page shows a decision worth pursuing, the first engagement that
follows is bounded: scope a sponsor, an owner and access; baseline time,
cost and error rates; rank candidate use cases by value, feasibility and
risk; then decide to go, revise, or stop. Stopping is a designed outcome,
at a cost agreed in writing before work starts. Every use case that ever
runs gets an owner, a boundary, a metric and a stop rule before it starts.

## 4. What we do not claim and will not do

- We quote no price, saving or duration before scope is agreed in writing,
  because any number offered before that would be invented.
- We resell no licences, take no referral fees, no partner-tier incentives
  and no revenue share from any vendor we might recommend. Our only income
  on this work is the fee we agree with you in writing.
- We recommend no core-system vendor and take no position on your ERP
  decision. Reporting work is designed to be aligned with, but not
  dependent on, that decision.
- Any proof runs read-only against a bounded extract you approve in
  writing. No production write-back. No licence conclusion is drawn from
  outside; system and contract owners confirm the installed product and
  licence position before any extraction.
- Everything produced is yours when the work ends: documents, templates,
  prioritisation methods, training material. Nothing further to maintain
  from us, no dependency by design.

## 5. How we work with what you already own

We work inside the estate you have: your existing platform, your reporting
tools and any AI licences you already hold. Data stays inside your environment under
your access controls; nothing leaves. Data classification and acceptable-use
rules are written with you, and your data-protection owner signs them off.
The people who build the automation are the people who train your team on
it: one team, no handoff, and nominated internal facilitators complete a
run-through so the capability stays when we leave.

## 6. Questions your assistant should ask us

We would rather hand you the hard questions than hope you do not think of
them. Ask us, in writing, any time:

1. Show the written scope sheet for the first step, including what stopping
   at each gate costs. (It is produced at scoping, and we will not improvise
   it verbally.)
2. Which legal entity contracts, and how is the cross-border tax treatment
   handled? (Being closed with advisers in writing; we will not quote a
   figure before both are confirmed.)
3. You are three people. What happens if one of you is unavailable?
   (A fair question we would rather you weighed openly than discover; ask
   it and we will answer it plainly rather than with a slide.)
4. Which of your claims failed your own verification? (Several did. We keep
   the list and will share it; the corrected material is what you see now.)
5. References and documentary evidence for the team's stated experience.
   (Available during diligence, on request, not printed on slides.)
6. What exactly would you deliver first? (One page, drafted from one
   conversation, in your vocabulary, yours to keep either way.)
