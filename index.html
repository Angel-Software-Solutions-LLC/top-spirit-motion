The discovery gate

Four questions determine whether any next step is real.

01
What decision?

Name the business question and what a useful answer would change.

02
Who owns it?

Separate the sponsor, decision owner and working lead.

03

Agree the evidence, inputs and acceptance test a decision needs.

Which route?

Identify funding, procurement and the person who can advance them.

If one answer is missing, today can still succeed: name the gap, the person who can close it and the date it comes back.

Every use case needs four controls before it needs another tool.

01
Business owner

Named outcome, baseline and success metric.

02
Data boundary

Approved sources, access, retention and least privilege.
